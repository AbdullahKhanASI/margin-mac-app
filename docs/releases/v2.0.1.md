Margin 2.0.1 makes the trackpad edges opt-in per touch, so they never get in the way.

## Changed

- **Hold fn or ⌥ (Option) to use a gesture.** Edges and corners now only respond while you hold fn or ⌥ as your finger
  touches the trackpad. An ordinary touch near the edge no longer changes the volume, brightness or anything else. Once
  a slide has started you can let go of the key. Hold ⇧ as well for Fine Control.
- **New setting: Use without Fn or Option key.** In **Settings → Edges**. Turn it on to use gestures with no key held,
  the way 2.0 worked. It replaces *Require fn or ⌥*.
- This applies to existing installs too: after updating, gestures need fn or ⌥ until you turn the new setting on.
- The menu shows a reminder under the trackpad diagram, and the setup guide tells you to hold the key while you try
  each gesture.
- Dismissing a timer alarm with a tap still needs no key.

## Install

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdullahabwisk/margin-mac-app/main/install.sh | bash
```

Or download **Margin-v2.0.1.zip** below. See the [README](https://github.com/abdullahabwisk/margin-mac-app#install) for
details.

Requires macOS 14 or later.
